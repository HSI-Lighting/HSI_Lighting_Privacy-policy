Pagify Desktop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Pagify Desktop is an app from HSI Lighting for reading and editing PDF documents. It runs entirely on your computer.

In short

  • Pagify Desktop does not send your documents, or anything else, over the internet.
  • There is no account to create and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • The few things Pagify remembers are kept in a folder on your computer, listed below. You can delete them at any time.

Your documents

Documents you open in Pagify are read, edited and saved on your computer, and are never uploaded. Recognising text in scanned pages (OCR) and spell checking also run on your computer.

What Pagify keeps on your computer

Pagify keeps these items in its settings folder. On Windows, this is %APPDATA%\Pagify.

  • Recent documents: the names and locations of documents you have opened, so you can open them again quickly.
  • Session logs: a record of the commands you ran and the messages Pagify showed during each session. It is there to help find the cause of a problem. Logs can include file names and text you typed into a command, but never passwords or passcodes. Only the 20 most recent sessions are kept; older logs are deleted automatically.
  • Signatures: signatures you draw or upload, so you can place them again without redrawing them.
  • Saved text: text such as a name or an address, kept only when you choose to save it. Pagify does not remember what you type into forms unless you save it.
  • Your dictionary: words you add to the spell checker.
  • Recorded scripts: sequences of commands you choose to record and save.
  • Preferences: settings such as light or dark appearance, and the fonts you have set to be outlined.

Passwords and certificates

Passwords you type to open or protect a document, passcodes for locked content, and the passwords for signing certificates are used only for the task you are doing. Pagify does not save them and does not write them to the session logs. When you sign a document with a certificate, Pagify reads the certificate file you choose and does not keep a copy of it.

Connections outside your computer

  • Updates: Pagify looks for a newer version in a set folder and reads a version number from a file there. It sends no information. If that folder is synced by a service such as Dropbox, that service's own privacy policy applies to the syncing.
  • Web links: when you click a web link in a document, Pagify opens it in your default web browser. From then on, your browser's and the website's own privacy policies apply.
  • Printing: pages you print are sent to the printer you choose.

Sharing

We do not receive data from Pagify Desktop, so we have nothing to sell or share. If you send us a session log or a document so that we can look into a problem, we use it only for that purpose.

Deleting your data

To delete everything Pagify keeps, close Pagify and delete the %APPDATA%\Pagify folder. Uninstalling Pagify removes the app but does not delete this folder, so delete it yourself if you want it gone. To clear only the recent documents list, type clearhistory in Pagify's command box.

Children

Pagify Desktop is not directed at children and is not designed to appeal to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
